Published Work

Springer · March 2024

Classification and Diagnosis of Thyroid Disease Using XGBoost and SHAP

XGBoost classification model for 3 thyroid conditions with SHAP-based explainability. Identified TSH, T3, and T4 as primary diagnostic indicators (65% combined importance). Applied SMOTE inside cross-validation folds to address 3:1 class imbalance. A leakage audit established a 72.97% majority baseline, so the reported 85.1% reflects signal learned from hormone panels rather than from label-derived features.
